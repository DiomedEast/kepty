Why Kepty is different
I

A portrait, not a highlight reel

This is not a baby album. Baby albums are about the child. Kepty is about you; who you were, what kept you up at night, what you got wrong, what you loved without knowing how to say it. That is what they will want when they are old enough to ask.

II

On your phone or your watch

Leave a voice message from the park. Drop a note from your watch on the way to school. Write in the car, on a walk, at midnight. Kepty is always in your pocket. The moment you want to capture never announces itself.

III

Words, photos and videos

Write when something moves you. Add a photo of the moment. Record a video of yourself talking to them. The vault holds all of it; text, image, and voice; until the day it opens.

IV

They do not know it exists

The child knows nothing until their 18th birthday. No performance. No audience. Just you, writing honestly, knowing they will only read it when they are old enough to understand.

V

Write whenever you feel it

Write at 2am after a hard day. Write when they say something you never want to forget. No schedule, no pressure; just write when the moment calls. We only send one reminder a year, on their birthday, to capture who you are right now.

Privacy policy

Your entries are yours. We are simply the keeper.

Who we are. Kepty (kepty.app) is a digital time capsule platform operated by BonbelleGroup. We collect and store personal data solely to deliver the Kepty service.

What we collect. On this waitlist page we collect your email address only. When the product launches, we will also collect the entries (notes, photos, videos) you choose to add to your vault, and payment information processed securely through Stripe.

Why we collect it. Your email address is used solely to notify you when Kepty opens. We will send one email. We will not send newsletters, product updates, or marketing communications without your explicit consent.

Your entries. Everything you write in your vault is encrypted. We cannot read your entries. They are stored securely and released only to your designated child on their 18th birthday, or to you at any time upon request.

Your rights. Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@kepty.app.

Data retention. Waitlist email addresses are held until you unsubscribe or Kepty launches. Vault entries are held for the duration of your subscription and for 18 years maximum from the child's date of birth.

Contact. For any privacy-related questions: info@kepty.app
